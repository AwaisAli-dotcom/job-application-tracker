Privacy

We use your username and email to manage your account and password resets. Your applications, interviews, reminders, notes, and documents are stored so you can organize your job search. These records are visible only after signing in to your account; you can edit or delete individual records from the app.

Uploaded files are stored in private Cloudflare R2 storage. App hosting and data processing use Vercel and Neon PostgreSQL. Password-reset emails are sent through the configured email provider. Your password is stored as a secure hash, not as plain text.

Essential session and CSRF cookies keep you signed in and protect forms. This app does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. Please avoid putting passwords or other secrets in job-search notes or document links.
